8. ROC Compliance

Stay compliant, stay confident.

Every registered company in India must file its annual financial statements and corporate returns with the Registrar of Companies (ROC) each year. ROC Compliance is the legal requirement to report your company's financial activities and structural changes (like changes in directors, share capital, or registered office address) to the government. We simplify this complex legal chore by handling all your annual filings, board resolutions, and statutory registers. By staying fully compliant, your business avoids heavy government penalties, legal audits, and remains in good standing for future expansions or fundraising.

Our ROC Solutions Include:

  • Filing Annual Returns (Form MGT-7) & Financials (Form AOC-4)
  • Drafting board resolutions & minutes of meetings
  • Director addition / resignation filings (DIR-12)
  • Share allotment & transfer documentation
  • Maintaining statutory company registers u/s Companies Act

Why Direct Compliances are Critical:

  • Avoids ₹100/day per form penalty for late filings
  • Prevents directors from being disqualified u/s 164
  • Maintains "Active" company status on MCA database
  • Builds solid credentials for capital raising
